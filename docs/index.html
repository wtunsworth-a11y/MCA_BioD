A pilot of the Managalas project, funded by the European Union. Built to be handed on to a Papua New Guinean group that will grow a community of practice and identification expertise.

All images are from Ijivitari District, Oro (Northern) Province. Maps show the 3 km survey grid only, not exact camera positions. Elevations come from SRTM data. Candidate species, names and elevation ranges come from the IUCN Red List of Threatened Species (API v4).

Identifications are public GitHub issues in the project repository. Everything on this site can be exported and moved to another platform.
